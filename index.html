Pick an open spot and add your name. Jack and Jill teens and parents are welcome, and teen shifts count toward service hours. Please arrive 10 minutes before your shift. Questions: cnjheartsforsicklecell@gmail.com

What each role does

Setup crew
Get the room ready before the Red Cross team unloads.
  • Confirm the room is at 68°F
  • Open 9 tables and 25 chairs (no wheels) and line them up against one wall
  • Keep the entrance clear for the Red Cross driver
  • Check bathrooms for toilet paper and soap; put up signs
Registration desk
Check donors in beside the Red Cross staff member.
  • Mark names on the printed list with a highlighter as Red Cross scans IDs
  • Remind donors they need a photo ID
  • Point anyone who hasn't done RapidPass to the QR code
Canteen host
Look after donors after they give.
  • Keep the snack table neat and refill it from the Red Cross cart
  • Chat with donors while they rest
  • Tell the Red Cross team lead right away if someone looks unwell
Greeter & awareness table
Welcome people and share the mission.
  • Direct donors to registration
  • Hand out Hole in the Wall Gang Camp and sickle cell trait materials
  • Take photos (with permission) for #cnjheartsforsicklecell
Cleanup
Close out the room after the last donor.
  • Bag regular trash (the Red Cross removes medical waste)
  • Leave tables and chairs where the Red Cross used them
  • Make sure nothing is left behind
